# Sweet Crumbs by Hira

A small website for a home bakery in Faisalabad, made as the **Day 2 task** of the Tech SG Studio 3-Day Website Planning & Development guide.

## About the project
Hira runs a solo home bakery. Her orders came through Instagram DMs and WhatsApp, which became messy. This website shows her menu and gallery and lets customers send a complete order to her WhatsApp.

## Why I made it
To practice the real workflow of a web developer: understand the client first, plan, and then build.

## My strategy
1. **Day 1: AI as a client.** I used AI to play Hira and interviewed her about her business, customers, menu, delivery, payment, design, budget and deadline.
2. **Planning.** From the interview I made a requirement document, sitemap, content checklist and home page wireframe.
3. **Day 2: Build.** I built the website from those documents, starting with the Must Have items.

## Features
- Home, bestsellers, menu, gallery, order form and contact sections
- Order form that opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message
- Minimum notice days for each item, and no orders on Sundays or fully booked dates
- Delivery areas and address asked only when the customer chooses delivery
- Pastel pink, cream and gold design that works on phones

## Built with
HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no framework)
